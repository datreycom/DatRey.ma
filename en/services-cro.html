Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our partnership.

How does the partnership with DatRey work?

It all starts with a free audit of your current setup. Then, we define a custom strategy together (KPIs, budget, channels). Once approved, our team deploys the campaigns and provides regular reporting to track your ROI.

Is there a minimum budget required to work with you?

We adapt our strategies to your goals. However, for paid acquisition (Google Ads, Meta Ads), a minimum monthly ad budget of 5,000 MAD is recommended to gather enough data and optimize ROI quickly.

When will I see the first results?

For SEA and Social Ads, the first leads or sales usually arrive within the first week of broadcasting. For SEO, it's a long-term strategy: initial improvements are observed after 2 to 3 months of consistent optimization.
