Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

How long does it take to create my site? +
For a showcase site, count on average 2 to 3 weeks. For a multipage or e-commerce site, the timeline is generally 4 to 6 weeks, depending on complexity and the speed of mockup validation.
Will I own my website? +
Absolutely. Upon delivery and full payment, you are 100% owner of your site, its source code, your domain name and hosting. No hidden "rental" fees.
Can I modify the content myself later on? +
Yes! All our sites are delivered with an intuitive content management system (CMS). We train you to use it so you can be autonomous in adding articles, products or modifying texts.
