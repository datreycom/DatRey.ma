========== FAQ AGENCY ==========

Questions Frequent

Everything you need to know about our support.

How does the support with DatRey work?

It all starts with a free audit of your existing setup. Then, we define together a tailor-made strategy (KPIs, budget, channels). Once validated, our team deploys the campaigns and provides you with regular reporting to track the profitability of your investments.

Is there a minimum budget to work with you?

We adapt our strategies to your goals. However, for paid acquisition (Google Ads, Meta Ads), a monthly advertising budget of at least 5,000 MAD is recommended to gather enough data and optimize ROI quickly.

When will I see the first results?

For SEA and Social Ads, the first leads or sales usually come within the first week of broadcasting. For SEO, it's a long-term strategy: the first signs of progress are observed after 2 to 3 months of regular optimizations.
