Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with us.

What is the minimum budget to work with DatRey? +

We recommend an advertising budget (excluding agency fees) of at least 10,000 MAD/month for Google Ads or Meta Ads in order to have enough data to optimize and scale your campaigns effectively.

How soon can I expect a positive ROI? +

For paid acquisition (Google/Meta Ads), initial results typically arrive between month 1 and month 3 (testing and machine learning phase). For SEO, expect 3 to 6 months to see significant organic traffic impact.

Do I have access to my advertising accounts? +

Absolutely. Unlike many agencies, you remain the sole owner of your advertising accounts (Google Ads, Meta Business Manager) and your data. We work as a partner with admin access.

Do your contracts have a commitment period? +

No, we work without commitment periods for our recurring services (Ads & CRO). We believe that our campaign performance is the best guarantee of our long-term collaboration.
